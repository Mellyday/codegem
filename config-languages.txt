Must-have first
YAML: .yml, .yaml — tree-sitter-yaml
JSON: .json — tree-sitter-json (optionally JSON with comments: tree-sitter-jsonc)
TOML: .toml — tree-sitter-toml
INI/Properties: .ini, .cfg, .conf, .properties — tree-sitter-ini and/or tree-sitter-properties
Bash/SH: .sh, .bash, .ksh, also parse plain KEY=VALUE in many .env files — tree-sitter-bash
High-impact next
Dockerfile: Dockerfile, .Dockerfile — tree-sitter-dockerfile
Makefile: Makefile, .mk — tree-sitter-make
Terraform/HCL: .tf, .tfvars, .hcl — tree-sitter-hcl
XML: .xml — tree-sitter-xml
Nice-to-have (after the above)
PowerShell: .ps1, .psm1 — tree-sitter-powershell
CMake: CMakeLists.txt, .cmake — tree-sitter-cmake
Fish shell: .fish — tree-sitter-fish
Zsh: .zsh — tree-sitter-zsh
Nginx: nginx.conf — tree-sitter-nginx
EditorConfig: .editorconfig — tree-sitter-editorconfig
